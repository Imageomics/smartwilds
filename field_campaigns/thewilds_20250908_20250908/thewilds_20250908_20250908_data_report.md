### Observer 1 (notes/observer1.md)

Temperature progression: 8 deg at 9:00, 13 deg at 9:40, 16 deg at 10:47.

Observations:
- Weed with spines that get stuck on ungulates [photo: image.png]
- Controlled burning of grass -- becomes green again in two weeks
- Breeding season wrapped up
- Pere David's deer sitting in small groups of 2-3 or large groups of ~12, mostly lazing/resting [photo: image-1.png]
- Six Grevy's zebras observed
- Sichuan takin ("Tucket"): age determined by horns -- babies start growing horns at couple years old, grow straight until signature curve comes in. Breeding season over but two still showing interest [photo: image-2.png]
- Animals well habituated to vehicles. Vets ride feed trucks for darting. Takin not afraid of anything.
- Free ranging -- no fences at all. Animals given attention twice yearly with medical care, but not considered free-ranging by national park standards.
- Rhino -- solitary. White rhinos (two-horned) more social. Communication through dung piles; other individuals can determine age, reproductive stage.
