# April 23, 2026 — MOSAIC v2.1 scope pivot + team next steps

## Why we're pivoting

- Follow meeting with advisors - determined that synthesizing multi-modal traces from single-modality data is a significant contribution in its own right, and that the hardware feasibility claims can be supported by published benchmarks + one measured anchor instead of a full measured grid.
- Falling back to published inference benchmarks for edge tiers + one measured anchor on my field laptop. Jetson and Pi tests not feasible before deadline, also not critical to the paper's core contribution.
- This actually fits the paper's original motivation better: **synthesis methodology is the primary contribution; hardware is illustrative** — its role is to show what the Bill of Materials for SmartWilds v2 should be, and what to invest in next.

## Revised framing

- **Primary contribution:** MOSAIC synthesis methodology (Exp 1, 6.1, 6.2.A, 6.2.B, 6.3).
- **Secondary / illustrative:** predicted hardware feasibility → SmartWilds v2 BoM (Exp 3, 4, 6.4), with one physically accessible tier measured as the anchor (Exp 3.5).
- **Paper thesis:** how far off-the-shelf sensors + current SOTA models + methodology get us toward deployment-ready edge AI — and where our predictions stop, i.e. what to measure / buy next.
- §VII deliverable is no longer a generic blueprint; it's a **concrete BoM for SmartWilds v2** with per-tier node counts, predicted cost, flagged unmeasured assumptions.

## Experimental plan changes (v2.1)

- **Exp 3:** was "measured grid across 3 edge tiers." Now **predicted grid across 4 tiers** (Workstation, Jetson Orin Nano, Pi 5+Coral/Pi 4 CPU, field laptop) from published benchmarks. No device access needed. Every hardware claim in the paper carries a "(predicted)" qualifier except the laptop-tier anchor.
- **Exp 3.5:** was stretch goal (Jetson calibration if hardware accessible). Now **required** — field laptop is the sole physical measurement anchor, calibrating prediction error on the one tier we can touch. Without it, every hardware claim is pure simulation.
- **Drone LOSO:** 0/9 folds fail. Root cause: 37 flights are few and varied; per-mission heterogeneity dominates (each flight ≈ a different modality). LOSO is not an appropriate test for this modality. Dropped from the LOSO pass/fail table; explained in §VI.A footnote. Variance envelope (primary fidelity defense) still passes for drone.
- **Exp 6.4 scaling:** structure unchanged; output reframed as "predicted breaking points" not "measured."
- **§III.B methodology ladder:** unchanged structurally (measured → fitted → modeled → simulated → predicted); wording tightened to reflect one measured hardware tier instead of Jetson validation.
- **§VII blueprint:** every hardware claim qualified "(predicted)"; new concrete output is the SmartWilds v2 BoM.
